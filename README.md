## 📂 What You Will Find Here
This portfolio is constantly updated with deep-dive investigations across the entire cyber kill chain. Each report focuses on telemetry analysis, payload deconstruction ("Code Intuition"), NIST-aligned response, and detection engineering (Sigma/SPL). 

My investigations cover threat vectors such as:

*   Identity & Credential Access: Kerberos anomalies, Pass-the-Hash, LSASS dumping, and credential harvesting.
*   Endpoint Forensics: Process injection, persistence mechanisms, malicious macros, and process hollowing.
*   Network & Web Analysis: Reverse shells, C2 traffic analysis (PCAP), SQLi, and Log4Shell.
*   Lateral Movement & C2: SMB abuse, PsExec, and administrative share pivoting.
*   Detection Engineering: Crafting robust Sigma rules, Splunk SPL queries, and automating response workflows.
*   AI Security: Frameworks for auditing AI-generated detection logic and preventing hallucinations.
